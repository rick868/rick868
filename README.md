## Selected work

### 🌞 SolarQuest Academy
An immersive VR learning concept for solar-panel installation and maintenance, designed around competency-based learning. It brings renewable-energy education and hands-on practice together.

### 🎫 Campus Event Management System
A full-stack platform for creating events, managing registrations, verifying attendance with QR codes, and keeping participants engaged.

### 🌍 Flood Risk Mapping
A GIS project using spatial analysis and geospatial visualization to explore flood-prone areas in Kenya and support environmental insight.

### 📊 Project Management Club Platform
A digital home for project-management learning, networking, career development, and student-led initiatives.
